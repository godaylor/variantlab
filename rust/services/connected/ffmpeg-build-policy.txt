VariantLab M8 native codec policy

FFmpeg: 7.1.1
Enabled output: WebM / VP9 / Opus only
Required configure flags:
  --disable-gpl
  --disable-nonfree
  --disable-autodetect
  --enable-libvpx
  --enable-libopus
Explicitly excluded: x264, x265, fdk-aac, H.264, AAC and all non-free components.

FFmpeg is LGPL-2.1-or-later under this configuration. libvpx and libopus use
BSD-style licenses. The exact configure output is copied into the worker image
at /usr/share/variantlab/ffmpeg-build-policy.txt and exposed by readiness.
